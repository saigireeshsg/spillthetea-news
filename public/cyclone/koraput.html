🌀 Cyclone Arnab in Koraput: NO CYCLONE ALERT

Odisha · updated 31 Oct 2026, 3:20 PM IST

Arnab is over, but more rain is coming. Andhra is in recovery: power is back in 92% of affected villages (APEPDCL) and the Howrah–Chennai line is running again. IMD says a new low-pressure system from the Andaman Sea may become a depression and head for the Andhra coast, with heavy to very heavy rain on Nov 2–3. Rivers are still high in Ganjam and Balasore (Odisha).
