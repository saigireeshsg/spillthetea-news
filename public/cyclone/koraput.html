🌀 Cyclone Arnab in Koraput: NO CYCLONE ALERT

Odisha · updated 31 Oct 2026, 11:15 PM IST

Arnab is over, but a new storm is coming. IMD now says it may become a severe cyclonic storm (Murasu) and cross the south Andhra coast between Nellore and Narsapur on Nov 3 morning, with 100–110 km/h winds. That is stronger than first forecast, but still weaker than Arnab. Andhra is in recovery; in Ganjam (Odisha), the landslide search goes on.

Flash-flood alert; very heavy rain.

📰 Latest updates

  • Oct 31, 11:15 pm IMD's 5:30 pm bulletin raises the forecast for the new storm. The depression is about 980 km southeast of Visakhapatnam, 1,030 km southeast of Machilipatnam and 1,080 km east-southeast of Ongole, moving west-northwest at 30 km/h. It should be a deep depression by midnight, cyclonic storm 'Murasu' on Nov 1 and a severe cyclonic storm on Nov 2. It is expected to cross the south Andhra coast between Nellore and Narsapur on Nov 3 morning, with winds of 100–110 km/h gusting to 120 (earlier forecast: 80–90 km/h). Still weaker than Arnab. Odisha: orange (heavy to very heavy rain, 50–60 km/h winds) for south Odisha on Nov 3–4, most of all Rayagada, Koraput, Malkangiri, Gajapati and Ganjam; yellow alerts on Nov 1–2. One private forecaster on X says it won't become a cyclone; go by IMD. SACHET: no new red/orange district alerts for AP, Odisha, Telangana or Yanam at 11 pm, only AP lightning warnings. · IMD via Argus News & Pragativadi, Oct 31 (10:30 pm); NDMA SACHET

  • Oct 31, 8:20 pm New system update. APSDMA (MD Prakhar Jain): the depression may become a cyclone by Sunday (Nov 1) evening and reach the Andhra coast by Monday (Nov 2) evening. Isolated heavy to very heavy rain likely in central Andhra and Rayalaseema on Nov 2–3; coastal winds 45–65 km/h. Fishermen must not go to sea. Still no official landfall point; IMD expects it weaker than Arnab (80–90 km/h). Ganjam landslide (Gidipabali): bad weather stopped the Navy helicopter from landing, so the life-detection equipment is going by road and should reach early Nov 1; NDRF, ODRAF, Fire Services and Railways teams are searching in heavy rain.   • Oct 31, 7:15 pm The new Bay system is now a depression. IMD's 3 pm bulletin: it formed at 11:30 am about 330 km west-southwest of Sri Vijayapuram (Port Blair) and 300 km west-northwest of Car Nicobar, moving west-northwest. It should become a deep depression within 12 hours and a cyclonic storm after that (it would be named Murasu), with winds of 80–90 km/h gusting to 100 — weaker than Arnab's 150 km/h. It should reach near the Andhra coast by Nov 2 evening; PTI reports it may cross the AP coast on Nov 3 morning. No landfall point announced yet. APSDMA: rain on Oct 31 in Parvathipuram Manyam, Alluri Sitharama Raju, Konaseema, West Godavari and Prakasam; moderate to heavy rain for 3 days in south coastal AP and Rayalaseema; coastal winds 45–65 km/h. AP lightning alerts kept going out through the afternoon (SACHET). Odisha: the Rushikulya at Purushottampur (Ganjam) was still above normal at 7 am. · IMD via PTI (DT Next) & The Week, Oct 31; APSDMA via Asianet Newsable, Oct 31; NDMA SACHET
  • Oct 31, 6:20 pm New storm watch: IMD's 8:30 am update says the well-marked low in the southeast Bay should become a depression within 6 hours and may strengthen into a cyclonic storm over the next 24 hours (around Nov 1). It is forecast to move west-northwest and reach near the Andhra coast by Nov 2 evening; no landfall point yet. Very heavy rain likely in parts of coastal AP & Yanam on Nov 2–3. Odisha: yellow thunderstorm alerts for Ganjam, Gajapati, Rayagada, Koraput, Malkangiri (Oct 31–Nov 2), plus Puri, Khordha, Nayagarh and coastal districts on Nov 1.
