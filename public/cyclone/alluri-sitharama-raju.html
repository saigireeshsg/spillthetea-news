🌀 Cyclone Arnab in Alluri Sitharama Raju: NO CYCLONE ALERT

Andhra Pradesh · updated 31 Oct 2026, 8:15 AM IST

Arnab is over. It weakened into a low over Chhattisgarh on Oct 29. Andhra is now in recovery: power is back in 92% of affected villages (APEPDCL), the Howrah–Chennai line is running again, and families leaving relief camps get 25 kg rice. Rivers are still high in Ganjam and Balasore (Odisha).

Trees, poles down; some roads flooded.

🏫 Schools were shut Oct 27–28 (AP govt order)
