🌀 Cyclone Arnab in East Godavari: NO CYCLONE ALERT

Andhra Pradesh · updated 31 Oct 2026, 10:15 PM IST

Arnab is over, but a new depression has formed in the Bay. IMD says it may become Cyclone Murasu within 24 hours and near the Andhra coast by Nov 2 evening, possibly hitting land on Nov 3 morning. It should be weaker than Arnab (80–90 km/h winds). Andhra is in recovery; in Ganjam (Odisha), the landslide search goes on.

Storm over. Power fully restored (APEPDCL, Oct 30).

🏫 Schools were shut Oct 27–28 (AP govt order)

Ganjam landslide (Gidipabali): bad weather stopped the Navy helicopter from landing, so the life-detection equipment is going by road and should reach early Nov 1; NDRF, ODRAF, Fire Services and Railways teams are searching in heavy rain.   • Oct 31, 7:15 pm The new Bay system is now a depression. IMD's 3 pm bulletin: it formed at 11:30 am about 330 km west-southwest of Sri Vijayapuram (Port Blair) and 300 km west-northwest of Car Nicobar, moving west-northwest. It should become a deep depression within 12 hours and a cyclonic storm after that (it would be named Murasu), with winds of 80–90 km/h gusting to 100 — weaker than Arnab's 150 km/h. It should reach near the Andhra coast by Nov 2 evening; PTI reports it may cross the AP coast on Nov 3 morning. No landfall point announced yet. APSDMA: rain on Oct 31 in Parvathipuram Manyam, Alluri Sitharama Raju, Konaseema, West Godavari and Prakasam; moderate to heavy rain for 3 days in south coastal AP and Rayalaseema; coastal winds 45–65 km/h. AP lightning alerts kept going out through the afternoon (SACHET). Odisha: the Rushikulya at Purushottampur (Ganjam) was still above normal at 7 am. · IMD via PTI (DT Next) & The Week, Oct 31; APSDMA via Asianet Newsable, Oct 31; NDMA SACHET
  • Oct 31, 6:20 pm New storm watch: IMD's 8:30 am update says the well-marked low in the southeast Bay should become a depression within 6 hours and may strengthen into a cyclonic storm over the next 24 hours (around Nov 1). It is forecast to move west-northwest and reach near the Andhra coast by Nov 2 evening; no landfall point yet. Very heavy rain likely in parts of coastal AP & Yanam on Nov 2–3. Odisha: yellow thunderstorm alerts for Ganjam, Gajapati, Rayagada, Koraput, Malkangiri (Oct 31–Nov 2), plus Puri, Khordha, Nayagarh and coastal districts on Nov 1. Ganjam landslide (Gidipabali, Bhanjanagar): 12–13 still missing; Odisha is sending a Navy helicopter with a live-detection unit to scan the debris. · IMD via Argus News & India TV, Oct 31; OdishaTV & Pragativadi (Odisha govt/SRC), Oct 31
  • Oct 31, 5:20 pm Odisha landslides: 12 people are missing after a landslide hit hilltop Gidipabali village (Bhanjanagar block, Ganjam); about 25 people were there. One woman has been found. 160–170 Fire Services, SOG and NDRF personnel have been searching since last night. Odisha's toll is now at least 4: three of one family in a landslide at Raikia (Kandhamal) and one person in Gajapati. Floods remain only in Ganjam; the Rushikulya is below the danger mark and the Jalaka (Balasore) has receded. Ganjam: 1,05,622 people affected, 367 relief camps; Gajapati: 21,782 affected, 340 camps; cooked food going to all flood-hit villages. Also: IMD says the new Bay system should reach near the Andhra coast by Nov 2 evening. · Odisha SRC via OmmCom News, Oct 31; IMD via Telangana Today, Oct 31
