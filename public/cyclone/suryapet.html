🌀 Cyclone Arnab in Suryapet: ORANGE ALERT

Telangana · updated 28 Oct 2026, 2:20 PM IST

Arnab crossed the coast near Yanam overnight. It's weaker now and moving inland, but heavy rain and flash floods are still likely today and tomorrow in coastal Andhra, south Odisha and east Telangana.

The storm centre is about 254 km from Suryapet. Moving inland, north-west, towards south Odisha and Chhattisgarh

Heavy to very heavy rain today and tomorrow. IMD: heavy to very heavy rain, gusts up to 70 km/h (Oct 28).
