🌀 Cyclone Arnab in Suryapet: NO CYCLONE ALERT

Telangana · updated 31 Oct 2026, 5:25 PM IST

Arnab is over, but more rain is coming. Andhra is in recovery: power is back in 92% of affected villages (APEPDCL) and the Howrah–Chennai line is running again. IMD says a new low-pressure system from the Andaman Sea may become a depression and head for the Andhra coast, with heavy to very heavy rain on Nov 2–3. Rivers are still high in Ganjam and Balasore (Odisha).

Storm over. IMD: thunderstorms, lightning, gusts 30–40 km/h possible (Oct 30).

  • Oct 31, 5:20 pm Odisha landslides: 12 people are missing after a landslide hit hilltop Gidipabali village (Bhanjanagar block, Ganjam); about 25 people were there. Odisha's toll is now at least 4: three of one family in a landslide at Raikia (Kandhamal) and one person in Gajapati. Floods remain only in Ganjam; the Rushikulya is below the danger mark and the Jalaka (Balasore) has receded.
