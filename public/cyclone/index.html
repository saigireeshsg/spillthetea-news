📰 What's happening now

✅Arnab is over: it weakened into a low over Chhattisgarh (IMD, Oct 29). No cyclone alerts remain.
🌧️More rain coming: a new depression is likely to move towards the Andhra coast. Heavy to very heavy rain in coastal AP on Nov 2–3 (IMD). Fishermen stay ashore off AP Nov 1–4.
💡Power: 5,277 of 5,727 affected villages back (APEPDCL, Oct 30). Fully restored in Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Parvathipuram Manyam, Anakapalli, East Godavari, Kakinada, Eluru.
🔌Konaseema is slowest: about 8,000 poles fell there (CM). AP's target is full power restoration by this evening, Oct 31 (Energy Minister). Mobile network: 384 of 1,045 towers were still down on Oct 30.
🚆Howrah–Chennai main line fully open again since Oct 30, 3:45 pm (Ichchapuram–Jhadupudi bridge). Trains run at reduced speed.
📶Mobile network: 99.18% back in Andhra (DoT, Oct 31). Odisha: Ganjam, Gajapati, Kandhamal improving.
🍚Leaving a relief camp? AP gives 25 kg rice, an essentials packet and cash for immediate needs.
💰AP: ₹5 lakh for families of those who died; ₹1,500 per damaged coconut/oil palm tree; crop loss paid to bank accounts after field enumeration.
🕯️7 people died in Andhra Pradesh (CM, Oct 29). In Odisha, 3 died in a landslide (PTI).
🌊Odisha: Jalaka still in severe flood at Mathani Road (Balasore); Rushikulya at Purushottampur (Ganjam) easing to above-normal (CWC, Oct 31). Stay off river banks.
⚡Lightning warnings keep coming in AP and east Telangana. Stay indoors during thunderstorms; avoid trees, poles and open fields.

❓ Quick answers

My phone has no signal after Cyclone Arnab. What do I do?

Mobile service is back for 99% of Andhra (Konaseema ~92%). Keep "Automatic network selection" on in phone settings, or pick any network manually — intra-circle roaming lets you use another operator when yours is down. Emergency 112 works on any network.

Is Cyclone Arnab over?

Yes. IMD says Arnab weakened into a well-marked low over Chhattisgarh on Oct 29 and kept weakening. There are no cyclone alerts now, only lightning/thunderstorm warnings and river floods in Ganjam and Balasore (Odisha).

Is another cyclone coming to Andhra Pradesh after Arnab?

IMD says a low over the Andaman Sea is likely to become a depression around Oct 31 and move towards the Andhra coast. It forecasts heavy to very heavy rain in coastal Andhra and Yanam on Nov 2–3 and heavy rain in Telangana on Nov 1–2. It has not been named a cyclone. Fishermen should not go to sea off AP from Nov 1 to 4. Spill will update when IMD does.

Is Cyclone Arnab coming to Vizag or Hyderabad?

No. The storm is over. Visakhapatnam's power is fully restored (APEPDCL, Oct 30). Hyderabad had no cyclone alert.

Are trains cancelled due to Cyclone Arnab today?

The Howrah–Chennai main line is fully open again since Oct 30, 3:45 pm, at reduced speed. Visakhapatnam–Kirandul Night Express (18515/18516) was cancelled till Nov 4. See the train list on this page or call 139.

When will power come back in Konaseema?

AP's Energy Minister set Saturday evening (Oct 31) as the target for full restoration. Konaseema lost about 8,000 poles, so it is the slowest. Across APEPDCL, 92% of affected villages had power by Oct 30. Call 1912 for your area.

What compensation is given for Cyclone Arnab damage?

AP: ₹5 lakh to families of those who died; ₹1,500 per damaged coconut or oil palm tree; 25 kg rice, essentials and cash for families leaving relief camps. Crop losses are paid to bank accounts after field enumeration — make sure the village officials record your loss.

How do I claim crop loss for Cyclone Arnab?

Officials are doing field-level enumeration now. Be present when they visit, keep your land and bank details ready, and make sure your crop loss is recorded. No separate online form has been announced.

How many people died in Cyclone Arnab?

Andhra Pradesh reported 7 deaths (CM, Oct 29). In Odisha, PTI reported 3 deaths in a landslide.

Are schools open after Cyclone Arnab?

AP closed schools Oct 27–28 in coastal districts. We have not seen any new closure order; check with your school.

My family is in a cyclone-hit district. How do I check on them?

Call the district control room on its page here. In AP you can also WhatsApp Mana Mitra on 9552300009 (send 'Hi').
