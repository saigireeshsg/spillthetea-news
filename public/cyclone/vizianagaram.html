🌀 Cyclone Arnab in Vizianagaram: ORANGE ALERT

Andhra Pradesh · updated 28 Oct 2026, 1:25 PM IST

Arnab crossed the coast near Yanam overnight. It's weaker now and moving inland, but heavy rain and flash floods are still likely today and tomorrow in coastal Andhra, south Odisha and east Telangana.

The storm centre is about 243 km from Vizianagaram. Moving inland, north-west, towards south Odisha and Chhattisgarh

Landfall zone. Heavy rain, flooding, power cuts.
